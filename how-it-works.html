How It Works

Three Simple Steps to Sold.

No repairs, no showings, no waiting for a buyer's financing. Here's exactly how selling your house to us works — start to finish.

01

Tell Us About Your Property

Fill out our simple form or call or text us at 201-922-5422. Share a few basics — the address, the condition, and what's prompting the sale. It takes about two minutes, and there's no obligation.

Every situation is different, and we treat yours with respect and discretion.

02

Get a Fair Cash Offer

We review your property and the local market, then present a fair, firm, all-cash offer — usually within 24 hours. There's no pressure to accept, and we're always happy to walk you through how we arrived at the number.

No lowball games, no obligation. If it's not right for you, that's okay.

03

Close On Your Timeline

If you accept, you pick the closing date. We can close in as little as three weeks through a reputable local title company — or give you more time if you need it. We cover the closing costs, and you walk away with cash in hand.

You choose the date that works for your life, not ours.

What makes it different from listing?

When you list with an agent, you're often asked to make repairs, keep the home show-ready, host open houses, and wait months — all while the sale can still fall through if a buyer's financing collapses. Then commissions and fees come out of your proceeds at the end.

Selling to us removes all of that. We buy as-is, pay cash, cover closing costs, and give you a guaranteed sale on a date you choose. For homeowners who value speed and certainty, it's a fundamentally simpler path.
