Privacy Policy

How Flip Edge Realty collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Flip Edge Realty ("Flip Edge Realty," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or submitting your information to us, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including when you fill out a form, request a cash offer, or contact us by phone, text, or email. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number and email address
  • The address of the property you are inquiring about
  • Details about your property and your situation that you choose to share

We may also automatically collect limited technical information when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages viewed, through cookies or similar technologies. This helps us understand how our site is used and improve it.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and prepare a cash offer for your property
  • Contact you about your property by phone, text message, and email
  • Communicate with you about our services and follow up on your request
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website and services
  • Comply with applicable laws and protect our legal rights

SMS / Text Messaging & Phone Consent

When you submit your information through our website or otherwise provide your phone number, you consent to receive calls and text messages from Flip Edge Realty — including by automated means — at the phone number you provide, regarding your property and our services. Consent to receive marketing calls or texts is not a condition of selling your property or using our services.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. For help, reply HELP or contact us using the information below. After you opt out, you may receive a final message confirming your request.

We do not share your phone number or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in limited circumstances, including:

  • Service providers: with companies that help us operate our business and website (for example, hosting, communications, and customer-relationship tools), who are permitted to use it only to perform services for us.
  • Business transactions: in connection with evaluating, negotiating, or completing the potential purchase of your property, which may involve title companies, attorneys, or closing agents.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Flip Edge Realty or others.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site and understand usage. You can usually adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, though some features of the site may not function properly as a result.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices & Rights

You may:

  • Opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP
  • Ask us to stop contacting you by phone or email
  • Request that we update or delete the information we hold about you, subject to applicable law

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details below. Depending on your state of residence, you may have additional rights regarding your personal information under applicable privacy laws.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any site you visit.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of our website after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
